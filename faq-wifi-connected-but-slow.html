WiFi Aid · FAQ

Wi-Fi is connected but slow or keeps dropping: is it the router, DNS, my provider or one website?

WiFi Aid runs Wi-Fi, DNS and internet checks in one tap and tells you whether the problem most likely lies with your Wi-Fi, DNS, your internet provider or a single website, with the evidence behind every verdict.

  1. Run a Check: Wi-Fi, DNS and internet are tested in one tap and you get a verdict in seconds.
  2. Only one site won't load? Use Check a Website to see whether that one site is down or your whole connection is failing.
  3. Looks connected but keeps lagging? Deep Check keeps sampling to catch spotty connections and problems that come and go.
  4. Open the full evidence to see where it breaks: DNS, TCP, TLS, TTFB, HTTP, direct IP, IPv4 and IPv6, item by item.
  5. On mesh Wi-Fi, see which node you are on and whether you roamed since your last check, and compare past results in Check History.

You can also check from Apple Watch or a Home Screen widget. No account, ads, analytics or tracking; check history stays only on this device.
