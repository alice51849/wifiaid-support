What is WiFi Aid?

WiFi Aid is an iPhone app for slow or dropping Wi-Fi. It runs Wi-Fi, DNS and internet checks in one tap and tells you whether the problem most likely lies with your Wi-Fi, DNS, your internet provider or a single website, with the evidence behind every verdict. Deep Check keeps sampling to catch spotty connections that look connected but keep lagging. Check a Website shows whether one site is down or your whole connection is failing. On mesh Wi-Fi it shows which node you are on and whether it changed since your last check.

Who is WiFi Aid for?

Anyone whose Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping and who wants to know which part to look at: the Wi-Fi itself, DNS, the internet provider or one website. It also suits homes with mesh Wi-Fi, people whose problems come and go, and people who like to compare results over time, since check history is kept on the device. You can run a check from Apple Watch or see connection status on a Home Screen widget.

How does WiFi Aid charge?

WiFi Aid is one upfront App Store purchase. There is no subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country. An Android version is listed on Google Play.

How is WiFi Aid different from other ways to check a connection?

Based on what the listing describes: instead of a single result, it names the layer where the problem most likely is and shows the evidence item by item: DNS, TCP, TLS, TTFB, HTTP, direct IP, IPv4 and IPv6. Deep Check collects more stability samples for problems that come and go, the mesh view shows which node you are on and whether you roamed, and Check History lets you compare past results stored only on the device. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
